Scientific and software provenance

Scientific basis: Ren et al., Exploring Supersymmetry with Machine Learning,
Nuclear Physics B 943 (2019) 114613. DOI:10.1016/j.nuclphysb.2019.114613.
This project reconstructs the published Model-4 algorithm. It is not recovered
original author code. xBIT was inspected as a framework reference; the Python
scanner here is newly implemented rather than a copy of its old scan class.

physics/fh_hs_driver.F derives its HB/HS conversion from the HiggsSignals 1.4.0
example HBandHSwithFH.F. The upstream GPLv3-or-later notice is retained in that
file; the licence text is physics/LICENSE-HiggsSignals.txt. Compatibility and
input/output adaptations are described in the source and docs/reconstruction_assumptions.txt.

FeynHiggs, HiggsBounds, HiggsSignals, micrOMEGAs, and CalcHEP are third-party
software. Their archives are not redistributed inside this small source ZIP.
scripts/setup_physics.py retrieves hash-pinned archives listed in
configs/sources.lock.json; inspect and retain each downloaded package's own
licence, copyright notices, and scientific citation requirements.

micrOMEGAs 4.3.2 is retrieved from a fixed HEPcodes historical mirror commit.
The original official tarball checksum was unavailable. The exact mirror
commit, archive hash and limits of provenance verification are recorded in
the source lock and documentation. No claim of byte-identical official archive
authentication is made.

This independent variant uses raw physical inputs and only ln(omega) labels.
It does not recover unpublished author code. Any attached validation must be
read according to its stated scope; prior standardized-model scan efficiency
is not evidence for this variant. Cite the physics settings, source versions,
target policy, and reconstruction limitations with every scientific result.
